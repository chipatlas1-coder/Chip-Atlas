S355 is a non-alloy structural steel for welded constructions, frames and machine parts, with a minimum yield strength of 355 MPa in thin sections. It is still widely known by its old German name, St 52-3.

## Quality grades

The letters after S355 give the impact energy and the test temperature:

| Grade | Number | Impact energy KV, min. |
|---|---|---|
| S355JR | 1.0045 | 27 J at +20 °C |
| S355J0 | 1.0553 | 27 J at 0 °C |
| S355J2 | 1.0577 | 27 J at −20 °C |
| S355K2 | 1.0596 | 40 J at −20 °C |

"+N" after the name, as in S355J2+N, is a delivery condition (normalized or normalizing rolled), not part of the steel name; the number stays 1.0577. The current standard is **EN 10025-2:2019**, which replaced the 2004 edition.

## Old names: St 52-3 and St 44-2

| Old name (DIN 17100) | Today |
|---|---|
| St 52-3 (1.0570) | via S355J2G3 (1.0570, earlier EN 10025) to **S355J2 (1.0577)**; ArcelorMittal maps St 52-3 N to S355J2+N and St 52-3 U to S355J0 |
| St 44-2 | **S275JR** (1.0044) — an S275 grade, not S355 |

<Note kind="warn" title="'St 52' tube is not S355">
In hydraulics, "St 52" tube usually means a cold-drawn precision tube of **E355** to EN 10305-1 or -2 (formerly DIN 2391 St 52), for example E355+N, 1.0580. It is a different steel and standard from the plate and section steel S355 to EN 10025-2, with its own delivery conditions and properties. Specify the standard, not just "St 52".
</Note>

## Chemical composition

Maximum %, heat analysis, EN 10025-2, for products up to 40 mm:

| Grade | C | Si | Mn | P | S | N | Cu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S355JR | 0.24 | 0.55 | 1.60 | 0.035 | 0.035 | 0.012 | 0.55 |
| S355J0 | 0.20 | 0.55 | 1.60 | 0.030 | 0.030 | 0.012 | 0.55 |
| S355J2, K2 | 0.20 | 0.55 | 1.60 | 0.025 | 0.025 | — | 0.55 |

Above 40 mm the carbon limit of J0, J2 and K2 rises to 0.22 %; for sections, some makers' sheets apply 0.22 % from 30 mm. The carbon equivalent CEV is limited to 0.45 up to 30 mm and 0.47 from 30 to 150 mm.

## Mechanical properties

Minimum yield strength ReH falls with thickness:

| Thickness t (mm) | ≤ 16 | 16–40 | 40–63 | 63–80 | 80–100 | 100–150 | 150–200 | 200–250 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ReH min (MPa)** | 355 | 345 | 335 | 325 | 315 | 295 | 285 | 275 |

J2 and K2 continue to 400 mm with 265 MPa. Tensile strength Rm: 510–680 MPa below 3 mm, **470–630 MPa from 3 to 100 mm**, 450–600 MPa from 100 to 400 mm. Minimum elongation, longitudinal, t ≥ 3 mm: 22 % up to 40 mm, 21 % from 40 to 63 mm, 20 % from 63 to 100 mm; transverse values are 2 points lower.

## Weldability and machining

S355 has good weldability, owing to its low carbon content and the CEV limit. For machining it behaves much like a mild steel (Murray Steel Products); Meusburger recommends a better-machining steel such as 1.1730 for parts that need no welding. In ISO 513 it belongs to group P.
