16MnCr5 is a low-carbon manganese-chromium steel made to be case hardened. Carburizing adds carbon to a thin surface layer, which then hardens on quenching, while the low-carbon core stays tough. The result is a hard, wear-resistant surface on a tough core.

## Designations

| Name | Number | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 16MnCr5 | 1.7131 | Base grade |
| 16MnCrS5 | 1.7139 | Specified sulphur range 0.020–0.040 %; described by Deutsche Edelstahlwerke as particularly suitable for machining |

Approximate equivalent: AISI/SAE **5115**. No JIS equivalent could be confirmed.

The current standard is **EN ISO 683-3:2022**. It replaced the 2019 and 2018 editions and, before them, EN 10084:2008. Many mill data sheets still cite EN 10084.

## Chemical composition

Heat analysis, % by mass, EN ISO 683-3:

| C | Si | Mn | P max | S max | Cr | Cu max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.14–0.19 | 0.15–0.40 | 1.00–1.30 | 0.025 | 0.035 | 0.80–1.10 | 0.40 |

16MnCrS5 has S 0.020–0.040 %. The silicon minimum of 0.15 % appears in the EN ISO 683-3 based sheets of Ovako and Lucefin; EN 10084 gave only a maximum of 0.40 %.

## Hardness in the delivery conditions

| Condition | Hardness (HB) |
|---|---|
| +A, soft annealed | max 207 |
| +FP, treated to ferrite-pearlite and hardness range | 140–187 |
| +N, normalized | 138–187 |
| +TH, treated to hardness range | 156–207 (Deutsche Edelstahlwerke) |

| Step | Temperature (°C) | Notes |
|---|---|---|

Lucefin gives a surface hardness of about 57–64 HRC after carburizing and hardening.

<Note kind="warn" title="Core strength has no standard value">
The strength of the core after case hardening depends on the section size and the treatment. The makers' guide values disagree: for an 11 mm section, Deutsche Edelstahlwerke gives Rm 880–1180 MPa (blank-hardening guide values from the superseded DIN 17210), Lucefin 1030–1370 MPa. None of them is a requirement of the standard. Specify and test the core strength the part needs.
</Note>

## Weldability

Deutsche Edelstahlwerke rates 16MnCr5 as generally weldable. Lucefin recommends welding before carburizing, a preheat of 150–350 °C and stress relieving at about 600 °C.

## Machining

Machining is normally done before carburizing, in the +A, +FP or +N condition, so the hardness ranges above are what the tool sees. Where machinability matters, 16MnCrS5 with its controlled sulphur, or Ovako's calcium-treated version, is offered. After case hardening, finishing is done by grinding or hard turning. In ISO 513 the soft steel belongs to group P and the hardened case to group H.
