42CrMo4 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel for quenching and tempering. Its alloying lets it harden through larger sections than a plain carbon steel: compare its quenched and tempered strengths, given up to 250 mm, with those of C45, which stop at 100 mm. It is supplied soft-annealed, treated for shearability or quenched and tempered.

## Designations

| Name | Number | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 42CrMo4 | 1.7225 | Base grade |
| 42CrMoS4 | 1.7227 | Specified sulphur range 0.020–0.040 % for better machinability |

Approximate equivalents: AISI/SAE **4140** (some makers list 4140H or 4137), JIS **SCM440**. The governing standard is **EN ISO 683-2:2018**, which replaced EN 10083-1 and EN 10083-3 (2006).

## Chemical composition

Heat analysis, % by mass, EN ISO 683-2:

| C | Si | Mn | P max | S max | Cr | Mo | Cu max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.38–0.45 | 0.10–0.40 | 0.60–0.90 | 0.025 | 0.035 | 0.90–1.20 | 0.15–0.30 | 0.40 |

42CrMoS4 has S 0.020–0.040 %. As with C45, the silicon minimum comes with EN ISO 683; the old EN 10083-3 gave only a maximum of 0.40 %.

## Mechanical properties, quenched and tempered (+QT)

| Diameter d (mm) | Re min (MPa) | Rm (MPa) | A min (%) | Z min (%) | KV min (J) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 16 | 900 | 1100–1300 | 10 | 40 | — |
| 16 < d ≤ 40 | 750 | 1000–1200 | 11 | 45 | 35 |
| 40 < d ≤ 100 | 650 | 900–1100 | 12 | 50 | 35 |
| 100 < d ≤ 160 | 550 | 800–950 | 13 | 50 | 35 |
| 160 < d ≤ 250 | 500 | 750–900 | 14 | 55 | 35 |

For d ≤ 16 mm thyssenkrupp, Lucefin and Deutsche Edelstahlwerke give no impact value; Saarstahl prints 30 J. Ovako states that the steel through-hardens up to about Ø60 mm after oil quenching.

## Weldability

42CrMo4 is difficult to weld. thyssenkrupp warns of hardening in the heat-affected zone and the weld metal and calls for preheating, an interpass temperature chosen from the CCT diagram, slow cooling and a low-carbon Mo or CrMo filler. Deutsche Edelstahlwerke advises against welded structures and calls for post-weld heat treatment if welding cannot be avoided. Lucefin gives a preheat of 300 °C and stress relieving at 550 °C.

## Machining

thyssenkrupp notes that the machinability depends strongly on the microstructure, that is, on the delivery condition. For cutting-force calculations, ISCAR places annealed 42CrMo4 (200 HB) in its group 6 (kc1.1 = 1775 N/mm², mc = 0.24) and Seco uses 42CrMo4 as the reference of its group P5 (kc1.1 = 2020 N/mm², mc = 0.18). At a chip thickness of 0.2 mm the two give nearly the same kc; see [cutting forces](/fundamentals/cutting-forces/). For better machinability, makers offer 42CrMoS4 with its controlled sulphur content (Lucefin) or calcium-treated grades (Ovako's M-Steel).
